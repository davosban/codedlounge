Event Purpose

Splashy Flashy Fun & Fit Mansion And Pool Party With After-Party

Welcome to the Splashy Flashy Fun & Fit Mansion And Pool Party With After-Party, a premium multi-weekend event series designed to merge sports, fitness, recreation, and philanthropy. Our mission is to celebrate the importance of physical exercise and sports while raising vital funds to support children facing life crises; therefore we have merged exercise, sports, recreation and fundraiser together to achieve that goal.

This year, we are especially proud to acknowledge Soccer as our featured sport. As the 2026 World Cup approaches, we aim to capture the spirit of the tournament; promoting togetherness, culture, showcase diversity and national unity. This event isn't just for athletes; it is a space for families, friends, and people of all nationalities to bond, build good relationships and encounters, while celebrating their heritage through the beautiful event.

Event Highlights & Benefits

Aiming to bridge the gap between nations and promote tourism while fostering unity across cultures. Our goal is to drive greater endeavors, progressive ventures, and successful professional connections.

By attending this multi-weekend affair, guests contribute to a vibrant atmosphere that promotes:

  • Global Unity & Tourism: Bridging the gap between cultures and fostering progressive ventures among international businesses and colleagues.
  • Health & Wellness: Encouraging a healthy lifestyle through fun fitness, and the good tributes of exercise.
  • Recreational Activities: Promoting and showcasing the importance of sports and recreational activities which is good for health.
  • Free Fitness Boot Camp to promote health and wellness (every morning before the pool party.)
  • Diversity: Promoting diversity and it's good attributes.
  • Heritage: Showcasing the beauty of different nations and encouraging supporters to take pride in their culture.
  • Empowerment: Providing a platform for community engagement and the empowerment of neglected kids through sports and the arts.
  • Charity: Helping give aide to kids indeed under our chosen kid's life crisis.

The Cause: Supporting Children in Crisis

A significant percentage of the proceeds from this event will be donated to sports, arts, kid seminars, youth camps and other chosen kids causes.

How Your Contribution Helps:

Your support provides resources for youth camps, variety positive kids seminars, and structured athletic programs that offer:

  • Emotional Support & Healing: Using kid seminars, art, sports and youth camps as outlets for self-expression, helping children process their feelings and build self-worth.
  • Holistic Development: Your contribution goes to our chosen cause for children in crisis, kid seminars, youth camps, sports and art programs that will help vulnerable children stay on the right path.
  • Skill Building: Helping young people discover valuable life skills through art and sports that extend far beyond traditional education.
  • Mentorship & Leadership: Encouraging healthy lifestyles and fostering the next generation of leaders.
Our Financial Commitment:
  • Sports Scholarships: Funds will go toward kids' soccer teams and aspiring young athletes who lack the financial means to participate in professional or recreational sports.
  • After-School Programs: Supporting healthy activities and art programs that keep children engaged and productive.

Our fundraiser for chosen kids life crisis, kid's seminars, youth camps, kids sports and art activities, will...

  • help give good mentorship to kids.
  • build a positive self-image, self-worth, and self-love in children while building the inner strength they need to thrive.
  • use art as a tool for healing, thereby helping kids boost their confidence and truly understand their inherent value.

  • motivate the good arts and sports ambition of the kids.

At Coded Art Atelier & Events, we believe that utilizing events to drive fundraising and humanitarian causes is both an honor and a vital part of building a lasting legacy.

However, the mission to protect and empower the next generation requires a collective effort. We are committed to ensuring that no child is left behind, whether that means combating stereotypes and discrimination, supporting those facing life crises, or providing the infrastructure necessary for growth. From education to arts, to kids sports, youth camps, kids seminars and after-school programs, our goal is to ensure the well-being and upkeep of every child through orphanage infrastructures, free outlets, donations and free specialized facilities.

We recognize that we do not do this work alone. It is a blessing to see other ventures and individuals dedicated to the positive transformation of children's lives. We extend our deepest gratitude to ventures, endeavors and everyone established tackling these kids challenges and needs through sports, literacy, music, and recreation.

A special thank you and acknowledgment also goes to NGOs, foundations, orphanage owners, direct initiatives, companies, and private individuals who lead the way by donating funds, building sports and orphanage infrastructure, own and donate to food banks, host free youth camps and all other helpful channels that goes towards helping and solving the challenges and life crisis for children.
